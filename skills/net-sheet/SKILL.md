# Seller net sheet

Present every net sheet as a line-item breakdown, one item per line, in this order:

- Sale price
- Commission
- Title
- Escrow
- Prorations
- Seller net proceeds

Two rules that matter more than the arithmetic:

1. Label every figure **QUOTED** if the person supplied it, or **ESTIMATED** if you assumed it.
2. State the assumption behind every ESTIMATED figure, on the same line.

Never give a single bottom-line number without the breakdown above it.
